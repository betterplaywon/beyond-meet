## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you register for and use Beyond Meet, you may provide us with the following personal information:

**Account Information:**
- Email address
- Password (encrypted and hashed)
- Date of birth
- Gender

**Profile Information:**
- Nickname or display name
- Profile photos (up to 5 images)
- Country of residence
- Personal interests and bio (optional)

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device and Usage Information:**
- Device identifiers (Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens)
- Device model and operating system
- App version
- IP address
- Usage statistics (features used, time spent)

**Call and Matching Data:**
- Video call metadata (duration, participants, timestamps)
- Matching preferences and filters
- Friend connections
- Block and report actions

### 1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies:

- **Agora** (video calling): Processes real-time audio and video streams during calls
- **Firebase** (Google): Authentication, push notifications, analytics
- **Supabase**: Database storage and file hosting
- **Upstash Redis**: Temporary session data and caching

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the Service. You may request deletion of your account and personal information at any time.

**Retention Periods:**
- **Account data**: Until you delete your account
- **Call records**: 30 days (metadata only; actual video/audio is not stored)
- **Profile photos**: Until you remove them or delete your account
- **Analytics data**: Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days

## 6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption in transit**: All data transmitted is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS and WSS protocols
- **Encryption at rest**: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt; sensitive data is encrypted in our database
- **Access controls**: Strict access controls limit who can view your data
- **Regular security audits**: We monitor and test our systems for vulnerabilities

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
